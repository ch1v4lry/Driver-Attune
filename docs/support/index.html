Safety first. Set up the phone before driving and do not interact with it while the vehicle is moving. Driver Attune cannot detect every instance of distraction or drowsiness. If you feel tired, stop somewhere safe and rest.

Quick troubleshooting

The app cannot see my face

Place the phone in a stable mount with the front camera facing you. Keep your whole face visible, avoid strong backlighting, and check that Camera access is enabled in system Settings.

Warnings seem inaccurate

Mount the phone where it will remain during the drive, then calibrate while looking naturally toward the road. Recalibrate whenever the mount position changes.

Driving mode does not detect movement

Confirm that Driving mode is enabled and Location access is allowed while using the app. If you use automatic Driving mode, also allow Motion & Fitness access.

The alarm is silent

Check that the drowsiness alarm is enabled in Driver Attune and that the phone volume is audible. Test the alarm before beginning a trip.

How do I delete saved information?

Open the Summaries tab to erase the complete driving record. Open Records, then Drowsiness photos, to delete one photo or all photos. Deleting the app removes all locally stored information.

When contacting support

Please include, when relevant:

  • Your phone model and operating-system version.
  • The Driver Attune app version.
  • Whether the problem happens in portrait, landscape, or both.
  • The steps that reliably reproduce the problem.

Please do not email drowsiness photos or other sensitive images unless they are necessary and you are comfortable sharing them.
